Course Syllabus

01 — COURSE DESCRIPTION

Course Description

An introduction to the creation and styling of websites using HTML and CSS. Websites will include text, graphics, multimedia, and responsive layouts.

The course integrates artificial intelligence into the learning and development process. Students will learn how to use AI tools to explore concepts, generate examples, debug code, compare approaches, and support web development while maintaining responsibility for understanding and evaluating their work.

Prerequisite

IS 100B or IS 101, or instructor approval.

02 — STUDENT LEARNING OUTCOMES

By the End of the Course

  • Create HTML documents that render as expected in several different browsers.
  • Create HTML documents that include typical elements such as hyperlinks, lists, tables, images, and forms.
  • Use embedded and external CSS style sheets to control the appearance of webpages and websites.
  • Incorporate best practices for accessibility into the structure and styling of webpages.
  • Use validation and code-formatting tools to check webpages for structure, styling, and accessibility.
  • Construct a website consisting of a homepage and at least three additional pages.
  • Use AI tools to support learning, development, debugging, evaluation, and problem-solving.
  • Evaluate and explain AI-assisted HTML and CSS solutions.

07 — AI USE POLICY

Artificial Intelligence in This Course

Artificial intelligence is intentionally integrated into this course. Students may use course-approved AI tools to support learning, experimentation, problem-solving, debugging, code review, and web development.

The purpose of AI use is not simply to obtain finished code. Students are expected to develop the ability to understand, evaluate, test, modify, and explain AI-assisted solutions.

APPROPRIATE USE

Students May

  • Ask AI to explain HTML and CSS concepts.
  • Request examples and practice exercises.
  • Use AI to help identify possible coding errors.
  • Compare different implementation approaches.
  • Ask for feedback on code organization.
  • Use AI as a brainstorming and problem-solving partner.
STUDENT RESPONSIBILITY

Students Must

  • Understand submitted HTML and CSS.
  • Test AI-assisted code.
  • Evaluate whether suggestions are appropriate.
  • Correct errors rather than blindly accepting generated output.
  • Follow assignment-specific AI instructions.
  • Follow CSN academic integrity requirements.
Important

AI use does not remove the requirement that submitted work represent the student's own learning and that the student be able to explain the work.

11 — ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Academic Integrity

Students are expected to follow CSN's Student Academic Integrity Policy. Presenting the work or ideas of others as one's own, cheating on exams, unauthorized collaboration, or other violations of academic integrity may result in academic penalties.

Because AI is intentionally incorporated into this course, AI use is governed by the course AI-use policy and by the specific instructions provided for each assignment and assessment.

Key expectation

Students must be able to explain the work they submit and must not misrepresent another person's work, ideas, or assistance as their own.
